# Intake

Run this once, when `STATE.md` doesn't exist. Interview the owner in batches of up to four questions, and invite them to paste what they already have (schedules, syllabi, lists) rather than retype it.

1. **What you're running.** The areas of their life they want help with, and one concrete target for each. Done when each area has a target.
2. **Fixed time.** Classes, work, commute, recurring obligations, sleep. Done when you can tell their free hours in a typical week.
3. **Support.** Who they'd turn to if things got bad. Done when you have one real person, or the owner declined.
4. **Cut.** Keep at most `ACTIVE_CAP` areas active. Everything else is parked.
5. **Write the files** in the formats in `CLAUDE.md`.
6. **First day.** Run `daily.md`.
